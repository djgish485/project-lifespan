# Systemic OSK vs SENS Repairs in Aged WT Mice (Head-to-Head)

Linked theories: [Epigenetic Information (Sinclair)](../theories/epigenetic_information.md), [SENS Damage Repair (de Grey)](../theories/sens_damage_repair.md)

## Hypothesis

In naturally aged wild-type mice, partial epigenetic reprogramming (OSK-style; cyclic) can reduce mortality hazard and improve multi-organ function without requiring a multi-class damage-repair stack; alternatively, a SENS-style stack (targeted repairs across damage categories) is necessary and/or safer for robust lifespan extension.

## Prediction (Measurable)

- Survival:
  - If epigenetic primacy holds: OSK-only yields a clear median lifespan increase and meaningful tail extension (e.g., improved 90th percentile), with acceptable tumor incidence.
  - If damage-repair primacy holds: the SENS stack matches or beats OSK on survival and/or safety; OSK may shift clocks/biomarkers without durable hazard reduction.
- Hazard: pre-register a target effect (e.g., OSK arm hazard ratio <= 0.85 vs vehicle; SENS stack hazard ratio <= 0.85 vs vehicle) and treat the head-to-head comparison as the primary discriminator.

## Essentials Protocol

- Model: C57BL/6J (or standard WT strain used across the project), mixed sex; aged ~20 months at randomization.
- N: >=40/arm (power based on expected hazard ratio; pre-register).
- Arms (as proposed in the debate):
  - Arm A: Systemic, cyclic partial reprogramming (e.g., AAV9-OSK or an equivalent controllable delivery), monthly short pulses.
  - Arm B: SENS-style multi-damage repair stack (illustrative): intermittent senolytic (e.g., D+Q), a mitochondrial gene-therapy or mitochondrial repair/support component, and a crosslink/AGE-breaking component.
  - Arm C: Vehicle control.
- Randomization/blinding:
  - Randomize at enrollment; keep husbandry standardized.
  - Blind frailty scoring and terminal pathology reads.
- Safety monitoring:
  - Pre-define an "unacceptable tumor risk" threshold (tumor incidence and/or tumor-related death).
  - Standardized necropsy + histopathology for all animals.

## Controls

- Vehicle (Arm C).
- Single-agent components as pilot arms (optional) to avoid under-dosing the SENS stack.
- Positive control benchmark (optional): rapamycin, to calibrate study sensitivity.

## Endpoints and Analysis

- Primary:
  - Survival (median; 90th percentile) and mortality hazard (pre-specified survival model).
- Secondary:
  - Frailty index (longitudinal).
  - Epigenetic clocks (blood +/- liver) at fixed timepoints (interpret as secondary, not decisive).
  - Terminal histopathology (blinded): tumors, fibrosis, aggregates/lipofuscin/amyloid as applicable, mitochondrial morphology.
  - Immune function: optional pathogen-challenge sub-study (pre-specified) to test for infection tradeoffs.
- Analysis:
  - Pre-register primary comparisons (OSK vs SENS; each vs vehicle) and multiple-comparison handling.
  - Record all-cause vs tumor-specific mortality attribution.

## Cost/Time/Ethics

- Timeline: 18–30 months depending on cohort age at start and endpoints (survival requires long follow-up).
- Cost: likely high six figures to low seven figures depending on vector production, pathology, and N.
- Ethics: humane endpoints; early stopping rules for toxicity/tumor signals.

## Discriminator

This is a direct head-to-head discriminator:

- If OSK (Arm A) beats the repair stack (Arm B) on survival/hazard with acceptable safety, that supports epigenetic reset sufficiency in normal aged mice.
- If the repair stack (Arm B) beats OSK (Arm A) on survival and/or has materially better safety (tumor incidence), that supports damage-repair primacy or at least a damage-gated reset.
- If clocks move in Arm A but hazard does not, that supports the "biomarker/cosmetic reset" critique.

## Falsifiers

- OSK improves clocks/markers but does not improve hazard/survival vs vehicle under a pre-registered analysis.
- OSK requires unacceptable tumor incidence to achieve any survival benefit.
- The SENS stack yields no survival benefit beyond vehicle (suggesting the chosen stack is not a fair test and must be redesigned before drawing theory-level conclusions).
