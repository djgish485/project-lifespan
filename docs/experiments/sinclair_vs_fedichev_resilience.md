# Decoupling Epigenetic Reset from Resilience Dynamics

## The Crucial Question

Does epigenetic age reversal *cause* resilience improvement, or is resilience the primary variable that drives survival regardless of epigenetic state?

-   **Epigenetic Prediction:** Resetting the epigenome (OSK) will endogenously restore resilience and extend lifespan. Resilience interventions without reset are merely palliative.
-   **Resilience Prediction:** Improving recovery dynamics (Rapamycin/Exercise) is sufficient to extend lifespan. Epigenetic reset without dynamic stabilization may be cosmetic or fail to extend life.

## Protocol

**Subjects:** ~200 aged (20-month) wild-type C57BL/6J mice.
**Design:** 4 arms, randomized, blinded, n ≥ 50 per arm.

| | Resilience Dynamics Improved | Resilience Dynamics NOT Improved |
|---|---|---|
| **Epigenetic age reversed** | **Arm A:** Cyclic OSK + resilience training | **Arm B:** Cyclic OSK + sedentary/standard housing |
| **Epigenetic age NOT reversed** | **Arm C:** Rapamycin + exercise + environmental enrichment | **Arm D:** Vehicle control |

**Measurements:**
1.  **Epigenetic Clocks:** DNA methylation age (baseline, 3-month intervals, necropsy).
2.  **Resilience Dynamics:** DOSI-equivalent recovery index (serial blood draws after standardized perturbation like mild LPS or cold). Key metric: Recovery half-time.
3.  **Primary Endpoint:** Median and maximum lifespan.

## Interpretation

-   **Epigenetic Information Wins if:** Arm B extends lifespan significantly vs Arm D, *and* shows spontaneous resilience improvement (reset causes resilience). Arm B ≥ Arm C.
-   **Resilience/Criticality Wins if:** Arm C extends lifespan significantly vs Arm D, *and* Arm B fails to extend lifespan or does so only by improving resilience as a side effect. Arm C ≥ Arm B.
-   **Independent Drivers if:** Both B and C extend lifespan via distinct mechanisms (B reverses clocks; C improves dynamics), suggesting neither is the sole "primary" cause.
