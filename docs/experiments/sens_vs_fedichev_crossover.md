# Damage Repair vs. Resilience Enhancement Crossover

## The Crucial Question

Does extending maximum lifespan require targeted damage repair (SENS), or is enhancing dynamical recovery capacity (Resilience) sufficient? Are damage and resilience causally separable?

-   **SENS Prediction:** Damage repair (Arm B) will extend maximum lifespan significantly more than resilience enhancement (Arm C). Resilience improvement is a downstream consequence of repair.
-   **Resilience Prediction:** Resilience enhancement (Arm C) will match or exceed damage repair (Arm B) in hazard reduction. Lifespan extension tracks recovery dynamics, not damage inventory.

## Protocol

**Subjects:** Naturally aged C57BL/6 mice (20 months start).
**Design:** 4 arms, N≥50 per arm.

| Arm | Intervention |
|---|---|
| **A** | Vehicle control |
| **B (SENS)** | Full damage-repair stack (senolytics + AAV-TERT + HSC transplant) |
| **C (Resilience)** | Pure resilience enhancement: Optimized exercise + hormetic stressors (hypoxia/17α-estradiol) — chosen to avoid direct damage clearance. |
| **D (Combined)** | Arm B + Arm C |

**Measurements:**
1.  **Primary:** Maximum lifespan (last decile survival).
2.  **Recovery Battery:** Rate of recovery from stressors (glucose, CBC challenge, immobilization) at 6-month intervals.
3.  **Damage Panel:** Senescent cell burden, telomere length, crosslinks.

## Interpretation

-   **SENS Wins if:** Arm B >> Arm C in max lifespan. Also if Arm B extends life without improving recovery dynamics (decoupling).
-   **Resilience Wins if:** Arm C ≥ Arm B in max lifespan. Also if Arm C improves dynamics and extends life without clearing specific damage markers.
-   **Synthesis if:** Arm D >> Arm B or C (superadditive), or if both extend life via distinct biomarker signatures.
